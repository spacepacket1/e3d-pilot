## Contestable design decisions

Before committing to an architecture, dependency, data-model, or API-shape choice where reasonable engineers
would disagree, call the `e3d_debate` tool with the decision and the context a model without repo access would
need. Summarize the verdict, the consensus level, and any dissent to me before proceeding. Do not use it for
simple lookups, formatting, or choices with an obvious answer; it is slow and costs money.
