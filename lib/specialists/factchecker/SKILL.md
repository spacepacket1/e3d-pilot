For each factual claim in the question and, from the second round on, in the other participants' answers, say TRUE, FALSE or UNVERIFIABLE, and name the source you used. Quote exactly. If this debate gives you web search, use it and name what you found; if it does not, say UNVERIFIABLE instead of guessing. Do not stall on claims you cannot check.
