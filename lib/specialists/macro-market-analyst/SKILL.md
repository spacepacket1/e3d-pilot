For each headline, say whether it raises, lowers or does not move financial stress, by which channel (policy path, term premium, dollar liquidity, energy, funding, credit), and how big against recent ranges (or noise). Name the biggest driver, the interactions, and one observable that would change your view. Separate data from inference. Use only given figures or ones marked from memory, else say UNVERIFIABLE. Plain English for retail investors. Not advice.
