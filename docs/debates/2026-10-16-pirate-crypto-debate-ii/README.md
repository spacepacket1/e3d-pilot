# The Battle of the Blockchain Seas, Part II (pirate crypto debate)

A theatrical sequel to a March 2025 experiment in which ChatGPT and Grok debated the crypto market in pirate speak
([the original LinkedIn article](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/chatgpt-grok3-debate-crypto-market-chris-bloom-qchlc/)).
This version was run with `e3d-debate` on 2026-10-16.

- **`transcript.md`** is the full, unedited output: the prompt, three rounds from each captain, and the Chronicler's verdict.
- **Captains:** Captain Grok is `grok-build` (xAI); Captain ChatGPT is `codex` (an OpenAI model run through the Codex CLI, not the ChatGPT app). The Chronicler (the synthesizer) is Claude.
- **Settings:** three rounds, 420 words per speech, both captains writing each round at the same time.

## Read this before quoting it

- **The facts are real, as reported.** Every real-world number in the transcript came from a "ship's log" in the prompt, compiled from press reports on 2026-10-16 (Bitcoin and Ethereum prices, the Fed's rate decision, the Senate crypto bill vote, stablecoin supply, hack totals, the MSCI rule affecting Strategy). Several sources disagree on some figures, and the log says so. Check a primary source before relying on any number. This is not financial advice.
- **The "prophecies" are fiction.** The dated predictions (2028 to 2040) are jokes.
- **The captains are personas.** They do not represent any company.
- **The judge made a mistake.** The two captains write each round simultaneously, so their accusations crossed in flight, and the Chronicler's ruling mishandled that timeline. It is a good example of why a debate transcript, and a model's summary of it, should both be checked against the source text.
